Begin with people

Why now

AI could give us more freedom, more useful capability and more time for one another. The choices we make together will shape that future.

Your adventure

Community hours

One verified hour of voluntary human contribution is one C-hour. The record helps recognise what people give and what grows from it.

Reciprocity
